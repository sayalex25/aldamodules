Adds planning dates and calendar/gantt views to Helpdesk tickets,
allowing teams to schedule and visualize ticket work in calendar and
Gantt chart views.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

After installing the module, Helpdesk tickets will include planning date
fields and new Calendar and Gantt views accessible from the Helpdesk menu.
